Tournament Rules

Please Read Carefully Before Registration

1. Registration Fee

Tournament entry fee ₹500/- per player hai. Registration fee submit karne ke baad verification process start hoga. Sirf verified players ko official player list me include kiya jayega.

2. Player Eligibility

Player ki minimum age 16 years ya usse zyada honi chahiye. Registration form me correct aur complete information deni hogi. Wrong information ya fake details submit karne par registration cancel ho sakta hai.

3. Player Selection & Verification

Tournament me maximum 64 players ko hi entry di jayegi. Registration karna player selection ki guarantee nahi hai. Registration ke baad player verification process complete hoga. Verified hone ke baad hi player official player list ka part mana jayega. Agar player final selection me nahi aata hai, to registration fee refund kar di jayegi according to tournament policy.

4. Organizer Rights

Organizer ko valid reason ke basis par player ko select ya unselect karne ka poora adhikar hoga. Organizer ka decision final mana jayega.

5. Online Registration Rules

Ye online registration process hai. Player ko apni correct personal information submit karni hogi. Fake identity ya wrong information milne par player remove kiya ja sakta hai.

6. Player Auction & Team Allocation

Verified players ka auction conduct kiya jayega. Auction ke baad jis team/owner ne player ko select kiya hai, player ko usi team ke liye khelna hoga. Auction ke baad team change allowed nahi hoga. Participate na karne par payment refund nahi ki jayegi.

7. Match Discipline

Abusive language, fighting, bad behaviour allowed nahi hai. Tobacco, smoking, alcohol ya kisi intoxicating substance ke under match khelna strictly prohibited hai.

8. Jersey Rules

Players ko organizer ki official jersey me hi match khelna hoga. Jersey related dispute ka final decision organizer ka hoga.

9. Match Decisions

Referee aur organizer ke decisions ka respect karna hoga. Kisi bhi dispute me organizer ka decision final hoga.

10. Privacy Policy

Player ki personal information sirf NEW ERA CUP 2026 tournament management, verification aur official communication ke purpose ke liye use ki jayegi. Player data kisi bhi unauthorized third party ke saath share nahi kiya jayega. Player ki privacy aur information security ka poora dhyan rakha jayega.

11. Medical & Safety

Player apni fitness ke liye responsible hoga. Injury ya health issue ki responsibility player ki hogi.

12. Tournament Special Note

NEW ERA CUP 2026 ek special football tournament hai jo har 5 saal me ek baar organize kiya jayega.

13. Player Commitment After Auction

Auction ke baad jis team owner ne player ko select kiya hai, player ko usi team ke liye khelna hoga. Auction ke baad team change allowed nahi hoga. Agar player bina valid reason ke tournament me participate nahi karta hai, to registration ya auction related payment refund nahi ki jayegi.

14. Player Agreement

Registration submit karne ke baad player ye confirm karta hai ki usne NEW ERA CUP 2026 ke sabhi rules aur regulations ko dhyan se padh liya hai aur unka palan karne ke liye agree hai. Rules violation hone par organizer ko necessary action lene ka adhikar hoga.

Final Authority

NEW ERA CUP 2026 organizers ke paas tournament rules ko interpret karne aur final decisions lene ka adhikar hoga.
